This release is about three things that were quietly not working. One was reported by a user, and looking into it turned up the other two.

- Channels First works again. The switch under Settings, Appearance had been writing your preference and nothing had been reading it, so turning it on did nothing at all. Your channels go back above your contacts, and it now explains that it applies when the list is showing All rather than a single type. Thank you to the person who reported this — it was not obvious from the outside that the setting was the broken part. Its name was also unclear or simply wrong in seven languages, and has been rewritten.

- Battery calibration can be reset. PommeCore learns a correction for your battery over time, so that the percentage means something on your particular cell. If that correction was learned from a bad reading, or you changed the battery, there was no way to clear it and the wrong percentage was permanent. Settings, Device Info, Battery now shows the reading your radio actually sent alongside Reset Calibration.

- Several explanations now exist in your language. Two passages of help text had only ever been written in English, and the Channels First switch had no explanation at all. Those are translated into all twelve languages, and the check that looks for untranslated screens had been missing a whole class of them, which is why they survived this long.
