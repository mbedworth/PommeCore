This release fixes two bugs our reviewers reported, and a third we found while fixing them. Thank you for the reports — both were findable from your descriptions alone.

- Your radio's frequency is no longer overwritten. After a Bluetooth disconnect the app fell back to a built-in 906 MHz default and could not tell it apart from a value your radio had actually reported. Opening the Radio settings and tapping Apply then reprogrammed the radio — frequency, bandwidth, spreading factor and coding rate together. Settings the app has not read yet are now marked as unknown, and Apply stays disabled until the real configuration has arrived from your radio.

- Automatic contact cleanup no longer interrupts you. Every time the radio made room for a new contact, the app opened a Device Error dialog — one per contact — for something entirely normal that you had asked it to do. Cleanup is now reported in a single short banner that dismisses itself, and you can switch it off under Settings, Notifications, Contact Housekeeping.

- Fifteen error and status messages had only ever been written in English, including the full-contact-storage warning and every channel import failure. They are now translated into all twelve languages, and a new check runs on each build so the next one cannot slip through unnoticed.
